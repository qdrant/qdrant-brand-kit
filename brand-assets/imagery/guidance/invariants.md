# Brand invariants for imagery

These hold everywhere. No other guidance can override them. The mascot and logo invariants live in `../../mascot/` and the brand kit; this is the part that applies to imagery.

Copied from Design Lab `brand-kit/guidance/invariants.md` on 2026-11-04.

## Text in generated images

Generated images never contain text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks. Words on a design are set in code on top of the image, following [`guidelines/`](../../../guidelines/) section 11.

## Mascot

The mascot and the realistic astronaut imagery are never combined, at any size (routing handoff, 2026-10-23).
