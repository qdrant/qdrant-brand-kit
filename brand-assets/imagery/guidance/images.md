# All images

Applies to every generated image, in any image style. Sent when writing image prompts and reviewing an image.

Copied from Design Lab `brand-kit/guidance/images.md` on 2026-11-04.

## Be faithful to the request

- If the person described a scene, that scene is the brief. Keep their subject, objects, action and setting, in their own words where possible.
- Never add characters, objects, effects or symbolism they didn't ask for. No glowing points, networks, constellations or other "data" imagery unless they asked for it.
- If they shared content without describing a scene (an article, a docs page, an event), choose the most concrete, recognisable scene that represents it: a real subject doing a real thing. Use at most one simple visual idea, never a stack of metaphors.

## Options

All options show the same subject doing the same thing in the same kind of place. Vary only the camera (a close-up on the key detail, a wide establishing shot, a dramatic low angle), the moment (just before, during or just after) and the framing. The first option is the most direct, literal version of the request.

## Style

The image style's reference images set the look (palette, lighting, materials, rendering). Describe the scene, never the style.
