# Design

How the Qdrant design system looks, and why. Every decision has a date. Add a line when a decision is made in the studio, and keep the open questions honest.

The values themselves live in [`primitives/`](../primitives/). This file says how the design system uses them.

## References

What makes four admired systems look the way they do, as values the component tokens can take (research, 2026-11-14):

- **Vercel (Geist):** edges are 1px rings (8% black, 14% white) instead of borders; shadows are layered at 2 to 6% with negative spread; actions are monochrome and blue only marks focus and links; neutral grays; tracking tightens with size (-2%, -4%, -6%); 32, 36 and 40px controls with 6px radius; concentric radii; 150ms transitions.
- **Supabase:** a whole theme from a few inputs (hue, surface tint, contrast, layer step); borders are the text color at an alpha set by one contrast value; very flat surfaces; fields sunk, choosers raised; small uppercase mono captions for table headers and menu labels; 9px tracked uppercase badges; accent only on the primary button, links, switches and focus; checkboxes monochrome.
- **Ramp:** one accent, on the primary button only; display type at weight 400 with no tracking; secondary text is the main color at 60%; warm neutrals; 1px borders at 10% and no shadows; nested radii (12, 8, 4).
- **Neon:** pure black page with cool grays; display tracking -0.04em and UI tracking -0.02em; small uppercase mono labels; square surfaces with pill buttons; 1px rings instead of shadows; the accent as a highlighter behind text; a 2px focus outline offset by 2px.
- **What they share:** depth from hairlines and background steps rather than shadows, quiet weights (never Bold), lighter secondary text, a scarce accent, and type that tightens as it grows.
- **Space, subtly** (Linear, SpacetimeDB, NASA Eyes, SpaceX, Resend, Supabase, Railway, Twingate; 2026-11-14): a cool blue-black page (ours already is), one light direction (a top light on surfaces), hairline instruments (horizons, orbit ellipses, dot grids, only in page sections), mission-control type (uppercase mono labels, fixed-width digits, real data only), weightless motion (expo ease-out, no bounce), translucent floating layers. Avoid glows and colored shadows, literal rockets and planets, all-blue palettes, nebula gradients, twinkling stars behind data and fake telemetry. Page textures should come from our own patterns (the Dash pattern reads as a star field).

### Second pass: structure, with numbers (2026-11-15)

Research for the studio's Variations page, from live CSS where it's public and from Mobbin screens: Supabase, Vercel, Neon, Exa, Mintlify, Cloudflare (Kumo), Ramp (its Ryu tokens), and a Mobbin sweep of about 30 more (Linear, Railway, Clerk, PlanetScale, Sentry, Stripe, Mercury, Attio, OpenAI Platform, Cursor, Perplexity, Framer, Retool, Hex, the xAI console and others).

- **Mintlify:** edges are 0.5px rings (black at 16%) with a 0.5px inner highlight; shadows are four stackable layers (`0 1px 2px -.5px` at 12% up to `0 16px 8px -4px` at 2%); one 6px base radius generates the rest, and inner radius = outer radius − padding; no uppercase anywhere.
- **Cloudflare (Kumo):** neutral surfaces 1 to 4% apart; 1px rings at 10%; its signature card is a tray holding an inner panel with matching radii; 36px default controls at radius 8; focus on fields is a 1.5px dark ring, not the brand color; dialogs open near the top over a gray scrim; dashed rules and a 45° hatch for empty things.
- **Ramp (Ryu):** radius 0 on buttons, fields, panels, menus, dialogs and tabs (4 only on menu items and badges); solid opaque borders; focus darkens the field's border, with no ring; body text at weight 300; roomy 64px table rows; long layered shadows only on floating layers.
- **Supabase (measured):** surfaces step +.025 in OKLCH lightness; borders are the text color at 7.5% (13.5% on controls); raised buttons stack a 4% top highlight, a 6% bottom shade and a 10% inner ring; fields are recessed (black at 12%); captions are 12px mono uppercase at +0.1em.
- **Vercel (measured):** page #000, surfaces #0a0a0a, one 4% step; a double ring (white at 14.5%, then 1px in the page color); radii 6 for controls and 12 for menus and dialogs; tabs are a 2px underline; light-mode shadows at 2 to 6%.
- **Neon (measured):** pure black page and black cards, separated by opaque gray borders alone; square cards with pill buttons; display tracking −0.04em, body −0.02em; a graph-paper grid behind canvases and hatching for idle periods.
- **Exa:** light-first and warm; buttons at radius 4; micro labels in mono, 9 to 10px, uppercase, +0.08 to 0.1em; dashed gridlines in charts.
- **The structural styles the sweep found:** a framed sheet (chrome one step darker than the content), hairline black, tinted near-black (the closest to ours), soft fintech (white with 5% hairlines and a faint shadow), dense grid, a tray around cards, raised top-lit plates, and Swiss square.
- **What reads as established:** borders at 5 to 14% (never 15% or more), two to four surface steps a few points of lightness apart, radius by role (small controls, larger containers, matching nested radii), 32 to 36px controls with 13 to 14px sentence-case labels at weight 500, one accent on one primary action, mono only for machine values, shadows only on floating layers, quick motion (100 to 200ms, a 0.97 to 0.98 press).
- **What reads as futuristic without becoming a gimmick:** mono uppercase micro labels for data, status dots, key hints, recessed fields against raised buttons, top-lit plates, a dot or line grid behind canvases (never under data), corner brackets on one focal element. What tips over: uppercase mono on buttons, corner marks on every card, glass over content, grids behind tables and charts, and any glow.

The variations built from this are in `studio/lib/variations.ts`, on the studio's Variations page: ten from the research, then Plated Black (Plated's look on Hairline's neutrals, Juan, 2026-11-15) and Deep (the default look, deeper on dark: the page at neutral-975, halfway from neutral-950 to black, and cards and the sidebar at neutral-950. Menus, fields and edges keep the default's steps, so they stand out more, Juan, 2026-11-16). Hairline's page moved from black to neutral-975 the same day; Plated Black keeps black.

## Still to explore in the studio

The space theme, one layer at a time. The first layer is decided (above): light from above, telemetry captions, weightless motion and glass dialogs. Still to explore: depth (tuning the background roles), texture (our own patterns in web sections only, such as the Dash pattern as a star field), display type (Mona Sans width and tracking for headings), and one or two signature details from the brand's own assets (a horizon, orbit lines, a dotted globe of Qdrant Cloud regions). No glow anywhere. Textures and instruments belong in web sections; components stay quiet. Each decision gets a line in the table above.
